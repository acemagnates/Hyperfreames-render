# VISUAL STORYBOARD: THE METAMORPHOSIS OF TIME (CHRONO-FRACTURE)

**Project ID:** OriginalSpot  
**Visual Aesthetic:** Metamorphic Glass & Obsidian  
**Artistic Mandate:** Deep Geological Tactile Reality & Mathematical Crystalline Refraction.

---

## 1. Visual Flow & Scenes

### Scene 1: "The Compression" (0.00s - 10.00s)
- **Aesthetic:** Massive dark basalt plates (`#232326`) squeeze inward, sandwiching a glowing core of molten magma (`#f33f0b`). Fine details of slate grain and drifting soot particles fill the space.
- **Shot Grammar:** Dolly-in through floating dust, slow tilt down, ending in an extreme rack-focus pull blurring the background plates to focus on a sharp crystalline node.

### Scene 2: "The Refractive Fracture" (10.00s - 20.00s)
- **Aesthetic:** A beautiful crystalline metamorphic glass shard floats in a high-contrast dark space. Dynamic optical flares (`#0ec2dd` and `#ffffff`) slide across its sharp facets as it orbits in 3D perspective.
- **Shot Grammar:** Slow 15-degree camera pan around the prism, capturing intense internal refraction shadow sweeps. Catastrophic camera push-in triggers crystal shattering at 20.00s.

### Scene 3: "The Crystallized Resolution" (20.00s - 30.00s)
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, architectural obsidian panels form a massive monolith. Delicate blue 1px blueprint UI lines, corner brackets, and coordinate data streams draw procedural readouts onto the frame.
- **Shot Grammar:** Dolly-out revealing scale of the obsidian monument, slow perspective 3D rotation, followed by gradual fade to pure black vault.

## 2. Palette & HSL System

- **Primary obsidian background:** `hsl(240, 12%, 3%)` (absolute solid depth)
- **Compressed shale plates:** `hsl(240, 6%, 15%)` (textured basalt panels)
- **Energy crack accent:** `hsl(14, 95%, 48%)` (molten sulfur magma)
- **Refractive light core:** `hsl(188, 88%, 46%)` (chrono-cyan specularity)
- **Specular peak flare:** `hsl(0, 0%, 100%)` (pure white reflection)
- **UI readouts:** `hsl(240, 4%, 45%)` (neutral muted slate)

---

## 3. Transition Details

1. **Clip 1 -> Clip 2 (Macro Rack Focus Morph):** Squeezing plates blur out, while a sharp foreground crystal shard scales in, morphing from glowing orange to cold cyan core.
2. **Clip 2 -> Clip 3 (Prismatic Shatter-Wipe):** Exploding crystal prism generates a blinding cyan-white screen flash, dissolving shards into structured monolithic panels.
