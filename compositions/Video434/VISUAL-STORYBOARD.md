## Clip 01 — THE HOOK (0.00s - 10.00s)

### 1. Narrative & Emotional Intent
> **Emotion:** Quiet Astonishment / Grand Hubris
> **Narrative Goal:** Establish the immense scale and initial confidence of the Biosphere 2 project, contrasting human technology with the wild desert soil.

* **Visual Dominance Split:** 65% Historical glass dome schematics / 35% Dark atmospheric shadows.

### 3. Set Design & Physical Depth Stack
* **L0: Environment (BG) [Z: -500px]:** Dark radial gradient background `#0e0d0b` to `#1b1713` with a subtle micro-noise scanline overlay at 3% opacity.
* **L1: Practical Light [Z: -200px]:** A slow-sweeping volumetric god ray simulating harsh sunlight cutting through dome glass.
* **L2: Hero Subject (MG) [Z: 0px]:** High-contrast historical photo of the eight biospherians in red suits, panning in 3D parallax with Courier Prime subtitle labels.
* **L3: Lens & FX (FG) [Z: +250px]:** 20% film grain overlay canvas + heavy vignette enclosing the frame boundaries + 4 L-shaped brass corner brackets pulsing in scale.

### 4. Motivated Lighting Physics
* **Primary Source:** Warm sepia-toned spotlight sweeping left-to-right from top-center (50%, -10%), simulating natural sun tracking.
* **Shadow Projection:** A deep 16px soft drop shadow on all text elements and historical photos, moving coordinates from (-8px, 12px) to (8px, 12px) dynamically.

### 5. Tactile Materiality & Textures
* **Primary Prop:** Archival photographic paper with deckled edges, showing slight silver halide chemical staining.
* **Atmospheric Noise:** Light dust particles drifting on the Z-axis in the volumetric beam.

### 6. Camera Vector & Parallax Plan
* **Camera Movement:** Orbital Reveal: container rotates `rotateY: -10deg -> +5deg` while panning slowly `translateX(-20px -> +20px)`.
* **Parallax Shift:** BG shifts by +10px, MG (photos and typography) shifts by -15px, and FG lens vignette remains anchored.

### 7. Anchor Coordination
* **Continuous Anchor:** The horizontal accent brass border at the bottom-center of the screen anchors the viewer, remaining stable through the transition.
