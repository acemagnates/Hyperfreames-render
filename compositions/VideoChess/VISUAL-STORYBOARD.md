# VISUAL STORYBOARD — VideoChess

## Logline
**Concrete Brutalism meets Sunset Silhouette** — a dramatic reconstruction of Magnus Carlsen's legendary Queen sacrifice in the 2016 World Championship. Solid basalt/concrete chess blocks stand starkly against a warm, blazing horizon gradient. No cheap HUD neon. Fine architectural pencil-grid blueprints, heavy volumetric shadow-drifts, and raw slate/stone dust bursts synced to the beat of Adele's Skyfall.

**Emotional arc:** Dread (0-10s) → Calculated Tension (10-20s) → Shock/Explosion (20-30s) → Majestic Triumph (30-40s) → Brutalist Checkmate (40-50s) → Cinematic Afterglow (50-68s).

**Palette:**
- Concrete Gray: `#2a2b2d`
- Deep Shadow: `#111213`
- Sunset Horizon: `linear-gradient(180deg, #ff7e5f 0%, #feb47b 40%, #111213 100%)`
- Blazing Amber: `#f39c12`
- Blueprint Graphite: `rgba(254, 180, 123, 0.2)`

## Clip 01 — ORIGIN (0–10s)
- **Setting:** Slow camera zoom-in (`scale` 1.0 -> 1.08) on a massive concrete plinth in a dark void, back-lit by a stunning orange-red sunset gradient.
- **Pieces:** Stark silhouettes of chess pieces lined up on the starting squares.
- **Graphics:** Subtle graphite pencil-lines forming coordinates. Monospace HUD details `BOARD STATE: INITIAL // RATING: 2850`.
- **Text:** Large title "THE SOUL OF CHESS" fades in with soft, heavy drop shadows.

## Clip 02 — TENSION (10–20s)
- **Setting:** The board tilts dynamically (`rotateX` 65deg -> 55deg) as first moves occur.
- **Action:** A concrete pawn moves forward. A clean graphite vector path draws from its starting point to its ending point.
- **Graphics:** Deep shadow vectors elongate as the sun "sets" dynamically.
- **Text:** "TENSION MOUNTS" in Cormorant Garamond, drifting vertically.

## Clip 03 — CLASH (20–30s)
- **Setting:** High-contrast low camera angle.
- **Action:** White Knight forks the Black Queen. As the piece slides into place, a massive dust burst (25+ tiny, high-contrast, fully opaque concrete debris particles) expands and floats away.
- **Graphics:** The camera shakes violently on the beat drop (t = 14.28s) using a spring-like multi-phase translate.
- **Text:** `TACTICAL DEVIL` appears in a bold serif font.

## Clip 04 — SHACKLES (30–40s)
- **Setting:** Extreme 3D perspective projection. The camera pans horizontally.
- **Action:** White Queen slides diagonally across the board. The vector move path glows with an intense graphite-to-gold gradient.
- **Climax:** White Queen lands on h6, next to the Black King. The move of a lifetime.
- **Graphics:** Volumetric light rays stream from behind the Queen.
- **Text:** `BRILLIANT (!!) Qh6+` in Oswald/Inter.

## Clip 05 — CHECKMATE (40–50s)
- **Setting:** Downward camera look at a 75deg tilt.
- **Action:** Black King is trapped. White Rook slides to h8, locking down the victory.
- **Graphics:** The captured Black King piece slowly rotates and falls flat, its shadow dissolving. A dense particle storm sweeps across the board.
- **Text:** `MATE IN 2. THE DIRT WINS.`

## Clip 06 — SILENCE (50–68s)
- **Setting:** The camera dollies back slowly.
- **Action:** The board and pieces return to a peaceful sunset silhouette.
- **Graphics:** Soft amber dust drifts lazily upwards.
- **Text:** "In chess, the struggle is everything." — Magnus Carlsen. Clean fade out to solid dark grey.
