# REFERENCES — VideoChess

Visual inspiration and mechanical reference for the Concrete Brutalist Sunset style:

1. **Concrete Brutalism:** 
   - Textured basalt and concrete plinth block designs.
   - Raw stone finishes with high-contrast shadows.
   - Graphite coordinates like hand-drawn architectural blueprints.

2. **Sunset Silhouette:**
   - Stark, dark shapes of pieces standing against a bright, warm amber/orange gradient background.
   - Rim lighting on the edge of the board and piece silhouettes.
   - Drifting warm particles.

3. **ChessFXs Style Sync:**
   - Speed ramping on moves (slow start, rapid slide, slow landing).
   - Elastic camera bounces and camera shake on the beat drop.
   - Graphic overlays displaying move annotations (e.g. `Qh6+!!` or `BRILLIANT`).
